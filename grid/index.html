We test the certifier on public agent benchmarks (τ²-bench retail, airline and telecom, and public customer-service agent traces): a cheaper candidate shadows a large model's traffic, and the certifier says where, if anywhere, it can take over. This page gives a high-level view of that work; results we are confident in go on the home page.
